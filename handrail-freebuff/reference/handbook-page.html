Allowance

How much you get

Everyone gets 25 days a year plus public holidays. Your balance resets on 1 January and up to five unused days carry over.

RequiredBook at least ten working days ahead.

Requests with less notice go to the account lead, not your manager.

  3. (03)

    Account lead
